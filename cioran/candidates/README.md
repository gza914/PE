# Image candidates

These are all the stock images screened for *The Trouble*, including the ones that didn't make the cut.
Every candidate came from Wikimedia Commons through a search filtered to **CC0 or public domain** only, so all
of them are free to reuse. `manifest.csv` lists each image's title, author, license, search term and source page.

| status | count | what's here |
|---|---|---|
| **used** | 10 | Full resolution in `../src/`. These are the shots in the film. |
| **shortlisted** | 10 | Full resolution (1920 px wide) in `shortlist/`. These were serious contenders I passed over. |
| **considered** | 266 | Everything else the searches returned. 205 have 330 px previews in `previews/`; the rest have only a source link, because their previews failed to download under Wikimedia's rate limit. |

- `contact_sheets/sheet_00.jpg` … `sheet_07.jpg` show every candidate that has a preview. Used shots come
  first, then the shortlist, then everything else, each labelled with its manifest id and status.
- `contact_sheets/graded_frames_from_render.jpg` shows frames from the film itself, with the grade, slit and type applied.

## Why the shortlisted images were cut

- **c003 Spanish Bay fog, c024 bare tree in fog, c072 fog-covered mountain forest, c066 black-and-white forest:**
  these were good, but too close to the fog forest and jetty I used. The opening needed only two such images.
- **c155 night trees with lamps:** a near-duplicate of c151, which I used. c151 has the lamp closer to the centre,
  so it survives the narrowing slit.
- **c118 hand with cigarette:** an alternate framing of c127. c127 has more darkness around the hand.
- **c106 abandoned living room:** strong, but too literal a picture of ruin for the restrained tone.
- **c032 ice on a window:** a nice abstraction, but its detail disappears once the slit gets thin.
- **x2 portrait with scarf:** her upward gaze reads as hopeful, which is the wrong note here.
- **x3 silhouette at an arched window:** a portrait-orientation frame that crops badly to a wide slit. The empty
  arch (c178) did the same job.
